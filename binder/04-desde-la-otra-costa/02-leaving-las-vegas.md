## Translation

The initial version of this will be one rendered on Aug 17, 2026.

However, I had previously rendered a translation of this, while under significant mental health strain and physical illness duress, while standing on a street in Buenos Aires in late November 2025.

Unsurprisingly, the notebook in which I wrote that translation later was badly water damaged; surprisingly, due to leaking weatherstripping in an aging modified VW Beetle.

However, I believe some of that translation survives. I will compare my 2026 rendition with whatever is left of the 2025 rendition; any modifications will be noted, For Science.


### hope springs eternal
So: first line, and Ms. Crow is getting down with allusions. `life springs eternal` is a variant of `hope springs eternal`, which apparently was first coined by Alexander Pope in his *Essay on Man*. We will need to track down whether this has been translated, and if so, whether the phrase exists; initial linguee corpus concordance searches indicate most translators shy away from the phrase by eliding it entirely. Understandably.

### best part
This is a double meaning that I'm not *sure* I can reproduce in Spanish. In English, `best part` can mean both 'the greater period of time' as well as 'the qualitatively superior portion of that period of time'. lol fuck.

Also the second half of the sentence is fucking *weird*, and *oddly specific*, which limits me from doing something funky like `pasé lo máximo de...`

fuck it I'm making a Quijote joke, yolo
